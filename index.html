🪞 The Mid Class Mirror Mid Anchor
Mid class consonants are incredibly stable anchors. Whatever tone mark icon is drawn over a Mid-class consonant, that exact requested tone sound matches up perfectly (Ek makes Low, Tho makes Falling, Tri makes High, Chattawa makes Rising).
🏴‍☠️ The Low Class Rebel Shift Frequency Jump
Low class initials act like rebels when modulated by tone mark tags. They shift the actual audio output frequency up by exactly one track setting higher than traditional rules: Mai Ek (่) forces a Falling tone outcome, and Mai Tho (้) forces a High tone outcome.
💥 The Dead Syllable Choke-Spike Pressure Rule
Unmarked Low Class consonants crashing directly into abrupt short vowels (like รัก) don't have safe exit space to fade out. This explosive sudden compression spikes internal mouth air pressure, launching the terminal pitch cleanly into the High Tone zone. Long vowels give it tracking room to climb up and slide back down (Falling Tone).
🍏 The Baseline Syllable Checklist Foundations
Live Ending (คำเป็น): Finishes natively in smooth long vowels or clear ringing nasal tracks (-m, -n, -ng). Sound waves continue carrying on smoothly.
Dead Ending (คำตาย): Chokes off hard into stop consonants (-p, -t, -k) or short vowel constraints. The sound wave hits an abrupt wall.
